Yellow is you. Blue is them. Every line shows what was said and what it means, big enough to read across a table. Parley figures out who's talking and flips the direction on its own.

How it works

Three taps to understanding.

1

Pick two languages

Yours and theirs. Swipe up the picker, choose from the world's most spoken languages, done.

2

Put the phone between you

Hit start. Either person talks. Parley hears which language it is and translates toward the other.

3

Read, reply, repeat

Translations pop up in bubbles as the words land. Turn on voice and Parley reads them out loud too.

Saves every conversation

Transcript and audio, organized by day. Play it back live, bubble by bubble, exactly as it happened.

Nothing to sign up for
