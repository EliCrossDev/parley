Install Parley on iPhone, Android or your desktop in two taps

2026-11-01 · by Frankie Rossi, 0xROSSI Digital

Parley lives at one link. Installing it just means giving that link an icon and a full-screen window.

iPhone and iPad

Open parley-murex.vercel.app/app in Safari. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add. Parley now opens full screen from its own icon. The first time you start a conversation, allow the microphone.

Android

Open the same link in Chrome. Tap "Install on your phone" on the home page, or use the browser menu and choose "Install app". Chrome puts Parley in your app drawer and on the home screen.

Mac, Windows and Linux

In Chrome or Edge, open the link and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or use the "Install on your phone" button on the home page, which installs on desktop too. Parley opens in its own window, which is handy on a desk when someone is sitting across from you.

Why not an app store?

Because you should not need an account, a download, or an update prompt to have a conversation. Installing from the browser gives you the icon and the full-screen experience with none of that, and updates arrive automatically the next time you open it.
