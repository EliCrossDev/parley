Why Parley is free, and what supporters get

2026-11-02 · by Frankie Rossi, 0xROSSI Digital

There is no meter running when you use Parley, so there is nothing to charge you for. Here is the honest economics.

Nothing meters

Speech recognition is done by your browser. Translation comes from public engines. Voices are an open-source model that runs on your device. Conversations are stored on your device. The only infrastructure is static hosting and a tiny payment endpoint. That is why the free tier is the whole product: unlimited live translation, every language, spoken playback, your last five conversations saved.

Pay what you want, once

If Parley earns a place in your pocket, you can chip in whatever amount you choose, one time, no subscription. Supporters keep every conversation instead of the last five, keep audio recordings until they delete them, can export transcripts, and get a Keep setting that auto-trims history to any number they like. The email you paid with unlocks any other device.

What we will never do

Sell your conversations, because we never see them. Add an account wall. Put live translation behind a paywall.

Questions? See the FAQ or just open Parley.
